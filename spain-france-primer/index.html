The 60-second brief

Different match, same idea: just enough to follow along and sound like you meant to be there.

01

It's a semi-final

Win and you're in the World Cup final on Sunday. Lose and it's over. This is the biggest game of the tournament so far — no second chances.

02

The two best teams left

Many people's pick for the final was this game — a "final before the final." Both have been barely beaten all summer.

03

It's a grudge rematch

Spain knocked France out at the same stage of Euro 2024 two years ago, sealed by a wonder-goal from a 16-year-old. France have been waiting to settle it.

04

A clash of styles

Spain keep the ball and slowly strangle you. France barely need it — they'll sit back and hit you with the fastest, deadliest attack in the world.

For the England fan

England play Argentina tomorrow in the other semi. So whoever survives tonight could be England's opponent in the final — and Spain are the side that beat England in the Euro 2024 final. Reason enough to pick a side.

The storylines

The drama you need

Drop any one of these and you'll sound like you've been across it all summer.

A final before the final Semi-final

On paper these are the two strongest teams remaining, so whoever wins tonight will be many people's favourite to lift the trophy. It's the tie everyone hoped wouldn't happen this early.

France want revenge for 2024 France

Spain dumped France out at the exact same stage of Euro 2024, when a 16-year-old Lamine Yamal scored a screamer to stun Les Bleus. France have carried that for two years. (Video below — it's the whole story.)

Mbappé vs Yamal The duel

The two poster boys of the sport, a generation apart. Mbappé at 27, chasing all-time greatness — against Yamal at 18, the heir apparent. Whoever grabs the night grabs the headlines.

Two Ballon d'Or winners, one pitch Star power

The world's best player award has gone to two men in this match: Rodri (Spain's midfield anchor) and Dembélé (France's in-form forward). It's basically a Ballon d'Or head-to-head.

Spain left out every Real Madrid player Spain

For the first time in their World Cup history, Spain picked no Real Madrid players at all — a Barcelona-heavy squad chosen purely on form. A genuine talking point back home. (Ironically, France's Mbappé plays for Real Madrid.)

Ball-hoggers vs counter-punchers Tactics

Spain will have most of the ball and try to pass France into submission. France are happy to let them, then strike in a flash. Whoever wins the midfield battle — Rodri and Pedri against France's engine — usually wins the match.

The VAR tournament ⚖ VAR

Video reviews have been the running controversy of this World Cup, overturning big calls at a higher rate than ever. Tonight's referee has a reputation for strictness, so expect stoppages, reviews and at least one argument before full time.
